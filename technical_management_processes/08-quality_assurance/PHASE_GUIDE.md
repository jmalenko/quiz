# Quality Assurance

> **IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 — Technical Management Process 6.3.8**

## Purpose
Provide assurance that work products and processes comply with predefined provisions and plans.

## Key Activities
- Plan quality assurance activities
- Evaluate processes against standards and plans
- Evaluate work products against requirements and standards
- Report non-conformances
- Ensure non-conformances are addressed

## Outputs (AI Generates)
- `QA_PLAN.md` — Quality assurance approach and activities
- `AUDIT_CHECKLIST.md` — Process and product audit criteria
- `NON_CONFORMANCE_LOG.md` — Issues found and resolution status
- `QA_REPORT.md` — Quality status and trends
