# Information Management

> **IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 — Technical Management Process 6.3.6**

## Purpose
Provide relevant, timely, complete, valid, and if required, confidential information to designated parties.

## Key Activities
- Identify information to be managed
- Define information storage and access mechanisms
- Maintain information items per their retention schedules
- Distribute information to stakeholders
- Ensure information security and confidentiality

## Outputs (AI Generates)
- `INFORMATION_PLAN.md` — What information, where stored, who accesses
- `DOCUMENT_REGISTER.md` — All project documents with status
- `COMMUNICATION_PLAN.md` — Who gets what information when
