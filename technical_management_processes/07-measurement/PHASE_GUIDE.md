# Measurement

> **IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 — Technical Management Process 6.3.7**

## Purpose
Collect, analyze, and report data relating to processes and products to support effective management and demonstrate quality.

## Key Activities
- Define measurement needs (what to measure, why)
- Select measures and indicators
- Collect measurement data
- Analyze and interpret results
- Report measurements to stakeholders

## Outputs (AI Generates)
- `MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md` — Metrics, collection methods, targets
- `METRICS_DASHBOARD.md` — KPIs and current values
- `MEASUREMENT_REPORT.md` — Periodic measurement results
