# Decision Management

> **IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 — Technical Management Process 6.3.3**

## Purpose
Provide a structured, accountable approach for identifying, characterizing, and selecting decision alternatives.

## Key Activities
- Identify decisions requiring formal management
- Establish decision criteria and weights
- Assess alternatives against criteria
- Make and record decisions
- Track decision implementation

## Outputs (AI Generates)
- `DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md` — How decisions are made (criteria, process)
- `DECISION_LOG.md` — All decisions recorded with rationale
- `TRADE_OFF_ANALYSES.md` — Formal trade-off evaluations
