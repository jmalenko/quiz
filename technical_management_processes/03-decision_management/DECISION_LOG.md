- **Decision**: Adopted IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 waterfall model with 11 phases
- **Rationale**: International standard, comprehensive lifecycle coverage including operation and maintenance, clear process IDs
- **Impact**: 14 technical processes + 8 management processes, maintenance feeds back into lifecycle
- **Decided by**: Product Owner
