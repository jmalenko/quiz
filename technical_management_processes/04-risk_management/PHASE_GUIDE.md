# Risk Management

> **IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 — Technical Management Process 6.3.4**

## Purpose
Identify, analyze, treat, and monitor risks continuously throughout the project.

## Key Activities
- Identify risks (technical, schedule, resource, external)
- Analyze risks (probability × impact)
- Plan risk responses (avoid, transfer, mitigate, accept)
- Monitor risk indicators
- Execute contingency plans when triggered

## Outputs (AI Generates)
- `RISK_REGISTER.md` — All identified risks with P×I scores
- `RISK_MITIGATION_PLAN.md` — Response strategies per risk
- `RISK_MONITORING.md` — Indicators and triggers
