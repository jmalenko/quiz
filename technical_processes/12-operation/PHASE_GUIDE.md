# Operation

> **IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 — Technical Process 6.4.12**

## Purpose
Use the system to deliver its services in the intended operational environment.

## Key Activities
- Operate system per operational procedures
- Monitor system performance and health
- Manage incidents and problems
- Provide user support
- Collect operational feedback for maintenance
