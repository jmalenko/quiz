# Governance — System Analysis

## Methodology

This process follows **ISO 31000 — Risk Management** for risk assessment.

Requirements coverage (traceability check) is performed by AI at the Detailed layer.

## High Level Format

Human provides:
- Risk assessment (identify risks, likelihood, impact, treatment)
- Go/no-go decision

## Detailed Format

AI expands into:
- Requirements traceability matrix (SR → design element → status)
- Risk details and mitigation strategies
- Feasibility confirmation
