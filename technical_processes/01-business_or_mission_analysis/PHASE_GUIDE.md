# Business or Mission Analysis

> **IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 — Technical Process 6.4.1**

## Purpose
Define the problem or opportunity, characterize the solution space, and determine the business case for proceeding.

## Key Activities
- Define the problem or opportunity
- Characterize the solution space
- Determine potential solution classes
- Assess trade-offs among solutions
- Provide the basis for life cycle decisions

## Inputs (Human Provides)
- `USER_INPUT.md` — Business context, problem statement, opportunity

## Outputs (AI Generates)
- `BUSINESS_ANALYSIS.md` — Problem/opportunity analysis
- `SOLUTION_SPACE.md` — Candidate solution classes
- `BUSINESS_CASE.md` — Justification for proceeding
- `FEASIBILITY.md` — Feasibility assessment
