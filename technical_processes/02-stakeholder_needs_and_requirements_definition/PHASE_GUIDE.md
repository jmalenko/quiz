# Stakeholder Needs and Requirements Definition

> **IEEE/IEC 12207:2017 — Technical Process 6.4.2**

## Purpose
Identify stakeholders, elicit their needs, and transform them into a formal set of stakeholder requirements.

## Key Activities
- Identify stakeholders and their needs
- Define stakeholder requirements
- Analyze and validate requirements
- Manage stakeholder requirements throughout lifecycle

## Inputs
- `../01-business_or_mission_analysis/BUSINESS_ANALYSIS.md`
- `USER_INPUT.md` — Stakeholder identification and needs

## Outputs (AI Generates)
- `STAKEHOLDER_REGISTER.md` — Who, role, interest, influence
- `STAKEHOLDER_REQUIREMENTS.md` — Formalized needs (STK-XXX)
- `SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md` — Measurable success measures
- `CONTEXT_DIAGRAM.md` — System boundaries
